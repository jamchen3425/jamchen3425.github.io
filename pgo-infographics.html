WORK ABOUT RESUME

Pokemon Go, Infographics

Pokémon GO is an AR mobile game created by Niantic/Scopely, Nintendo, and The Pokémon Company in 2016. Players can travel around the world locating, capturing, training and battling Pokémon.

While working at Snack Creative, I created infographics for social media that show content updates, upcoming events and more!
