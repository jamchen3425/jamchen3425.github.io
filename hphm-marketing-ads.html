WORK ABOUT RESUME

Harry Potter: Hogwarts Mystery, Marketing ads

Harry Potter: Hogwarts Mystery is a role playing mobile game developed by Jam City in 2018. Players get to immerse themselves the world of Harry Potter by creating their own character and attending Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Witches and wizards have the opportunity to choose their fate and build relationships with other Hogwarts students and staff.

I worked on the Creative Services, Marketing team to create statics and video ads for user acquisition through organic social and paid media. We had the opportunity to work with cast members including James and Oliver Phelps (Fred and George Weasley), Bonnie Wright (Ginny Weasley), and Evanna Lynch (Luna Lovegood), to promote the addition of their character to the game.
