WORK ABOUT RESUME

Disney Emoji Blitz, Social Ads

Disney Emoji Blitz a match-3 puzzle and collection game developed by Jam City. Players match to clear boards, collect emojis, and can even participate in special events to unlock limited edition content. They can even use their collected emojis in text messages!

I worked on the Creative Services, Marketing team to create statics and video ads for user acquisition through organic social and paid media. We had the opportunity to work with actresses including Rosalie Chiang (Turning Red), Auliʻi Cravalho (Moana) and more, to promote their respective emoji characters.
